# BUG-003 — Save action is inaccessible on 360 px mobile viewport

**Source ticket:** SUP-003  
**Severity:** Medium  
**Priority:** P2  
**Status:** Open  
**Escalation:** ENG-BUG

## Environment

- Chrome on Android
- 360 × 800 viewport

## Preconditions

- User is signed in.
- Profile settings page is available.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Open Profile Settings on a 360 px wide viewport.
2. Edit any profile field.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the form.
4. Attempt to save.

## Expected result

The Save button remains visible and reachable within the supported mobile layout.

## Actual result

The action container overflows horizontally and the Save button is rendered outside the visible viewport.

## Frequency

5/5 attempts at 360 px. Not reproduced at 390 px.

## Customer impact

Users on affected small mobile screens cannot complete profile updates without rotating the device or switching to desktop.

## Workaround

Rotate the device to landscape or use a larger viewport.

## Retest criteria

- Verify 320, 360, 375, 390, and 414 px widths.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow.
- Confirm Save remains keyboard/touch accessible.
